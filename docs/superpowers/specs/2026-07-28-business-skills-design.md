# Business Skills Design — Current Atomic Catalog

The business department owns nine atomic Skills:

1. `business-customer-lead`
2. `business-requirement-communication`
3. `business-opportunity-assessment`
4. `business-solution-quotation`
5. `business-negotiation`
6. `business-contract-signing`
7. `business-project-delivery-tracking`
8. `business-acceptance`
9. `business-renewal`

Acceptance checks deliverables, exceptions, remedies, and customer sign-off evidence. Renewal separately evaluates value and risk, prepares scope and quotation options, and tracks authorized follow-up.

Customer records use stable IDs, minimum contact data, valid contact basis, restricted content references, explicit human confirmation, and append-only evidence. Stored status never independently proves signature, delivery, acceptance, payment, or renewal. All packages live under `.agents/skills/`.
